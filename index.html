Odds and Pricing

Bookmaker Margin

A bookmaker's odds include a margin. If two equal teams are priced at 1.90 each, the implied probabilities add up to about 105%, and that extra slice is how the bookmaker earns regardless of the result.

Exchange Commission

An exchange doesn't build a margin into the price. On a busy market the same equal match might show prices close to 2.00 on each side. Instead, commission is taken from net winnings on each market. For a winning bet, you'll often keep more on an exchange even after commission, but that depends on the market, timing and liquidity.

A Worked Example

You stake PKR 1,000 on the winning team. At a bookmaker offering 1.90, your profit is PKR 900. On an exchange at 2.00, your profit is PKR 1,000 before commission, and after a few percent commission it's usually still above PKR 900. The numbers are illustrative, but the principle holds: the exchange's cost is visible and applies only when you win; the bookmaker's cost is hidden in every price.

Laying and Trading

Only an exchange lets you lay, and it's the biggest practical difference between the two. If you lay a team at 2.50 with PKR 1,000 and it doesn't win, you collect PKR 1,000. If it wins, you pay 1,000 × 1.50 = PKR 1,500, which is your liability. Laying gives you more ways to bet, and more ways to lose more than you expected, especially at high odds.

Because you can back and lay the same market, you can also trade during a match. If you back a team at 3.00 with PKR 1,000 and its price falls to 2.00, laying PKR 1,500 at 2.00 locks in about PKR 500 whatever happens. Parimatch offers cash-out on some bets instead, at a value it calculates. Cash-out is convenient, but the price includes the bookmaker's margin.

Liability: The Concept Bookmaker Users Need to Learn

If you've only ever used a bookmaker, one idea on BetPro needs special care: liability. With a bookmaker, your maximum loss on any bet is your stake. On an exchange, that's true for back bets, but not for lay bets. When you lay, you're acting as the bookmaker, and your potential loss depends on the odds.

This table is why many new BetPro users are surprised by a big loss after laying an outsider. The bet slip always shows the liability before you confirm, so read it every time. A useful rule is to decide the maximum you're willing to lose first, then set the lay stake from that: lay stake = maximum loss ÷ (odds − 1).

Exposure and Available Balance

BetPro shows an exposure figure: the most you could lose across all your open bets. That amount is held back from your available balance until the bets settle. Users coming from bookmakers sometimes think money has disappeared when they see a lower available balance mid-match. It hasn't; it's reserved for open bets. Once markets settle, exposure falls to zero and the balance updates.

Faraz uses BetPro. Before the match he backs Pakistan with PKR 1,000 at 2.40. Pakistan start strongly and the price drops to 1.70. Hassan uses a bookmaker. He backs Pakistan at 2.30 with PKR 1,000. When Pakistan start well, he's offered a cash-out of a certain amount. It's less than what an exchange trade would have locked in, because the cash-out value includes the bookmaker's margin, but it's one tap. He takes it.

Both approaches are reasonable. The exchange gives more control and usually a better price. The bookmaker gives simplicity. Neither changes the fact that most betting, over time, costs more than it pays.

Payment Agents and Fake Numbers

Both local desks and international brands attract scammers who pose as official payment channels. Common tricks include:

  • A "payment agent" who messages you first, claiming to process deposits for a famous brand.
  • A WhatsApp number in a group that claims to be "official BetPro support".
  • A message saying "our usual number is busy, send your deposit here instead" on a big match night.
  • Requests for your OTP or wallet PIN "to confirm the deposit".

The rules are the same everywhere. Use only numbers and payment methods listed on the official website. Never share an OTP or PIN. And confirm any new number through the official site before sending money.

Common Misunderstandings

"An International Brand Must Be Safer"

Size and advertising don't guarantee smooth withdrawals for Pakistani users. Read the terms and test a small withdrawal first, exactly as you would with a local desk.

"Exchange Betting Is Risk-Free Because You Can Trade"

Trading only helps when prices move your way. When they don't, a trade can lock in a loss instead. Laying can also create large liabilities.

"The Bonus Means I'm Betting With Free Money"

Bonus funds usually come with wagering requirements that keep you betting. Many bonuses are never converted into withdrawable cash.

"Offshore Licences Protect Pakistani Users"

An offshore licence applies to the company's home jurisdiction. It doesn't create rights you can easily enforce from Pakistan, and it doesn't make the service legal here.

Why the Model Matters More Than the Brand

Users often ask which brand is "best". A more useful question is which model fits the way you bet, and whether you can control your spending on it. If you like to trade during matches and bet against outcomes, an exchange-style platform suits that. If you prefer simple single bets and don't mind the margin, a bookmaker is simpler. Either way, the platform won't protect you from overspending. Your budget, your limits and your willingness to stop do that.

Signs Betting Is Becoming a Problem

  • Depositing more than you planned, or topping up mid-month.
  • Chasing losses with bigger stakes.
  • Borrowing, or using money meant for bills or family.
  • Hiding betting from people close to you.
  • Feeling restless or irritable when you can't bet.

If this sounds familiar, take a break, ask your desk or the brand to close your account, and talk to someone you trust. Doctors and counsellors can help, confidentially.

Account Security on Any Platform

Most lost balances come from stolen logins rather than bad bets. These habits protect you whichever platform you use:

  • Bookmark the official address and always log in from that bookmark, never from a link in a message.
  • Use a unique password that isn't used for your email, WhatsApp or wallet.
  • Change the starting password as soon as a desk gives you your ID.
  • Avoid unknown APK files. Fake "official apps" are a common way to steal logins.
  • Never share OTPs or PINs, with anyone, for any reason.
  • Log out on shared devices and check your statement regularly.

Budgeting When You Use More Than One Platform

Some users open accounts on several platforms to chase bonuses or better prices. That makes spending much harder to track. If you do it, keep a single monthly budget covering all platforms and one record of every deposit and withdrawal. At the end of each month, subtract total withdrawals from total deposits. That single number shows what betting really cost you, and it's often higher than people expect. The simplest protection is one platform, one budget and small, fixed stakes.

Accumulators and Special Markets

Bookmakers heavily promote accumulators: single bets that combine several selections, all of which must win. The potential payout looks huge because the odds multiply. So do the margins. With five selections, the bookmaker's edge is applied five times over. Accumulators occasionally land and make great screenshots, which is exactly why they're promoted. If you use them, keep stakes small.

Special markets such as top batter, total sixes or method of dismissal work the same way. Each has its own margin, often higher than on the main match market. On an exchange like BetPro, the focus is on match odds, sessions and fancy lines, though placing many session bets on one match can build up exposure in a similar way.

Influencers, Promo Codes and Advertising

International brands invest heavily in marketing across South Asia: sponsored posts, influencer promo codes and banner ads on streaming sites. A few things to keep in mind:

  • Influencers are paid to promote, and the "big wins" they show may be staged or selective.
  • Many promo codes pay the promoter a share of what their referrals lose.
  • A famous face in an ad doesn't protect your deposit or make a service legal in Pakistan.
  • Scam pages often use cricketers' photos without permission to look official.

BetPro desks mostly grow through word of mouth and WhatsApp, which carries its own risk of fake dealers. In both cases, judge a platform by its written terms and its record of paying withdrawals, not by its marketing.

Timing: Busy Match Nights

On big nights, like Pakistan vs India or a tournament final, both local desks and international brands get very busy. Deposits take longer to credit, withdrawals queue up after the result, and scammers are at their most active. Deposit well before the toss, keep receipts, avoid "new" payment numbers, and if you can wait, request withdrawals the next day rather than straight after the match.

Rules for Rain, Ties and Abandoned Matches

Cricket is full of interruptions, and each platform has its own rules for what happens to bets when they occur. Before betting on a match with a poor forecast, check:

  • Reduced overs: whether match odds stand and how session or total markets are treated if the over mark isn't reached.
  • Ties and Super Overs: whether the Super Over decides match odds, or whether a tie has its own settlement.
  • Abandoned matches: whether bets are voided and stakes returned.
  • Reserve days: whether open bets carry over when a knockout match moves to a reserve day.

On BetPro, the rules appear in the market or rules section of the site, and your desk can explain them. With a bookmaker, they're in the sports rules. Many disputes come from users assuming a rule rather than reading it.

A Note for First-Time Users

If you've never bet online before, don't start by comparing brands or bonuses. Start by deciding whether you want to bet at all, and how much you could lose without it affecting your family, bills or savings. Most regrets in betting come from moving too fast, not from choosing the "wrong" brand. Slow, small and written down is the safest way to start anywhere.
